Our Story

A house built on slowness.

Drift began as a small corner room with three chairs, one grinder, and a single decision — to serve coffee the way it deserves to be served. Without hurry. Without compromise.

Every cup that leaves our bar is the result of a quiet conversation between bean, water, and time. We source from farms we know by name, roast in small batches, and brew as if no one were waiting.

Elena Marchetti
Founder & Head Roaster

The Craft

Why people come back.

The difference is not loud. It is in the bean, the water, the temperature, the hand. It is the way a room is held. Four quiet promises shape every cup we serve.

01

Organic Beans

Sourced from family-run farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. Single origin, traceable, certified, and chosen one harvest at a time.

02

Handmade Brewing

Every pour over is timed by hand. Every espresso is dialled in each morning. Nothing leaves our bar that hasn't been tasted by the barista who made it.

03

Fresh Bakery

Sourdough, almond croissants, fig tarts — baked before the sun rises in our open kitchen. Made with butter from a single dairy two valleys away.

04

Cozy Atmosphere

Warm light, slow music, oak benches, and a corner for one. A place that invites you to stay a little longer than you planned.
